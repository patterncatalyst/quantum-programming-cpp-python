This site's chapter arc, coverage, and level of rigor draw on a small set of
reference works. They are reference works, not sources: no text or code from any of
them is reproduced here. Each link points at the publisher or journal, not a reseller.

| Title | Author(s) | Publisher | Year | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [*Quantum Computation and Quantum Information*](https://www.cambridge.org/highereducation/books/quantum-computation-and-quantum-information/01E10196D0A682A6AEFFEA52D53BE9AE) | Michael A. Nielsen, Isaac L. Chuang | Cambridge University Press | 2010 | The standard graduate text; its linear-algebra chapter matches Appendix A |
| [*Quantum++: A modern C++ quantum computing library*](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0208073) | Vlad Gheorghiu | PLOS ONE | 2018 | The paper behind the C++ library this book's primary track uses |
| [*Programming Quantum Computers*](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/programming-quantum-computers/9781492039679/) | Eric R. Johnston, Nic Harrigan, Mercedes Gimeno-Segovia | O'Reilly Media | 2019 | A hands-on, code-first tour of the quantum primitives |
| [*Dancing with Qubits*](https://www.packtpub.com/en-us/product/dancing-with-qubits-9781838827366) | Robert S. Sutor | Packt Publishing | 2019 | A from-the-math-up introduction for programmers |
| [*Quantum Computing: An Applied Approach*](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-030-83274-2) | Jack D. Hidary | Springer | 2021 | Foundations plus algorithms across several platforms, with a math toolkit |
